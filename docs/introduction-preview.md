# 插件与 MCP 简介预览 / Introduction preview

## 中文

**由李二牛开发，让 AI 更懂你的 Garmin 训练。**

这是一款开源的 DeepSeek Harness 插件与 MCP 连接器，支持中国区和国际区账号。让 AI 查询完整活动与分段、睡眠、心率和 HRV 等数据，并在你确认后创建、删除训练课表或取消日历排期，更方便地分析训练、调整安排。

**账号由你掌握，密码不交给 AI。** 使用推荐的 Garmin 官方网页登录流程，密码和验证码只在官方页面输入，不交给 AI 或开发者。登录会话凭据保存在本机，并通过私密文件权限保护；你授权查询的运动与健康数据，会返回给你选择的 AI 客户端用于分析。

## English

**Developed by Li Erniu (李二牛), connecting Garmin training to your AI assistant.**

This open-source DeepSeek Harness plugin and MCP connector support Garmin China and International accounts. Let your AI assistant explore full activities and splits, sleep, heart rate and HRV, then create or delete workouts or cancel calendar entries after your confirmation—making training reviews and adjustments easier.

**You control your account. Your password is not shared with AI.** With the recommended Garmin browser sign-in, passwords and verification codes are entered only on Garmin’s official page, not given to AI or the developer. Session credentials are stored locally with restricted file permissions. The fitness and health data you authorize the connector to retrieve are returned to your chosen AI client for analysis.
